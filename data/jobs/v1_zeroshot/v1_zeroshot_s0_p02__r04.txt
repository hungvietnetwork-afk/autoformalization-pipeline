### P117
In triangle $ABC,$ $\cot A \cot C = \frac{1}{2}$ and $\cot B \cot C = \frac{1}{18}.$  Find $\tan C.$
Answer: 4

### P118
How many complex numbers $z$ such that $\left| z \right| < 30$ satisfy the equation
\[
  e^z = \frac{z - 1}{z + 1} \, ?
\]
Answer: 10

### P119
If $8 \tan \theta = 3 \cos \theta$ and $0 < \theta < \pi,$ then determine the value of $\sin \theta.$
Answer: \frac{1}{3}

### P120
Let $\bold{v} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\bold{w} = \begin{pmatrix} 11 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}$.  Find the area of the parallelogram with vertices $\bold{0}$, $\bold{v}$, $\bold{w}$, and $\bold{v} + \bold{w}$.
Answer: 23

### P121
Convert the point $(\rho,\theta,\phi) = \left( 4, \frac{5 \pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$ in spherical coordinates to rectangular coordinates.
Answer: (2, -2 \sqrt{3}, 0)

### P122
Let $\mathbf{A}$ be a $2 \times 2$ matrix, with real entries, such that $\mathbf{A}^3 = \mathbf{0}.$  Find the number of different possible matrices that $\mathbf{A}^2$ can be.  If you think the answer is infinite, then enter "infinite".
Answer: 1

### P123
Given that $(1+\sin t)(1+\cos t)=5/4$ and
$(1-\sin t)(1-\cos t)=\frac mn-\sqrt{k},$
where $k, m,$ and $n$ are positive integers with $m$ and $n$ relatively prime, find $k+m+n.$
Answer: 27

### P124
Let $\mathbf{R}$ be the matrix for reflecting over the vector $\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}.$  Find $\mathbf{R}^2.$
Answer: \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
